Clip Desk Poster: Terms of Service

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Clip Desk Poster is a private tool run by the owner of the Clip Desk North accounts. It publishes that owner's own video clips to that owner's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign up for or use it.

Use

Only the account owner uses the tool, and only with accounts they own or are authorised to manage. Everything posted must follow each platform's own terms and community guidelines.

Content

The account owner is responsible for everything the tool posts. The tool only uploads content the owner has reviewed and approved.

No warranty

The tool is provided as is, without any warranty. It may stop working if a platform changes its API.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from each platform's connected-apps settings.
