Clip Desk Poster: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Clip Desk Poster is a private, single-user tool. It publishes the owner's own clips to the owner's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts.

What it stores

  • The OAuth tokens each platform issues for the owner's account. They are kept on the owner's own computer, in a file only that user can read.
  • Basic account details returned by the platforms (such as the display name), used only to confirm which account is connected.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect data about other people or about the people who watch the videos.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not use cookies, analytics or tracking.

Data use

Data obtained from TikTok, Google/YouTube and Meta is used only to upload and publish the owner's videos to the owner's accounts, as each platform's API allows.

Deletion

To delete everything, revoke the app in each platform's connected-apps settings and delete the token files from the owner's computer. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

Contact

Questions: reach Clip Desk North through its YouTube channel or TikTok profile.
